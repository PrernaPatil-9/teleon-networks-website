About Teleon Networks

Teleon Networks Pvt Ltd is an IT & Infrastructure Solutions provider. We manage and support business IT infrastructure through proactive monitoring, maintenance, troubleshooting, security, backup and on-site or remote support.

IT & Infrastructure Solutions

Complete IT solutions, delivered as one service

Our work spans the full lifecycle of business IT: designing and installing infrastructure, configuring networks and security, migrating and managing cloud platforms, supplying hardware, and maintaining everything afterwards under support and AMC arrangements.

Services can be taken individually or combined, so a business can start with a single requirement — a network installation, a cloud migration, an AMC — and extend the scope as their infrastructure grows.

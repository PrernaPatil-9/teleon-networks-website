Service overview

Keeping business connectivity visible and accountable

We monitor leased line and business broadband connectivity, track bandwidth, latency, packet loss and jitter, coordinate with ISPs and report on uptime and incidents.

The scope covers link and gateway monitoring, download/upload bandwidth and performance analysis, ISP coordination and complaint follow-up, dual ISP, failover and load balancing monitoring, and alerts on link-down, high latency and packet loss.

Monthly reporting covers uptime, bandwidth utilization, downtime, ISP performance and incident history — so connectivity issues are tracked, escalated and closed out with a clear paper trail.

  • Dual ISP, failover & load balancing

Capabilities

How we work

  1. Baseline — record current links, bandwidth and ISP details.
  2. Instrument — enable link, gateway and performance monitoring.
  3. Alert — notifications on down, high usage, latency, packet loss.
  4. Coordinate — raise and follow up ISP complaints and escalations.
  5. Report — monthly uptime, bandwidth, downtime and ISP performance.

Service overview

CCTV solutions at a glance

We deliver IP/analog CCTV installation, DVR/NVR setup, camera configuration, cabling, testing and maintenance — with clean routing and proper labelling at every camera point.

CCTV engagements cover the full site delivery cycle — understanding camera coverage requirements, planning camera positions and cable routes, installing cameras and DVR/NVR, configuring recording and remote viewing, then testing and documenting every point before handover.

Work can be taken as a standalone installation or as part of a larger IT / security project, and can be delivered across single or multi-site environments.

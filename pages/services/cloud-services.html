Service overview

Cloud services at a glance

We help businesses move, protect, manage and monitor their IT infrastructure on reliable cloud platforms, using leading providers per client requirements.

Cloud engagements at Teleon Networks cover the full lifecycle — from assessing what you run today, to planning the right platform and architecture, migrating workloads with minimum disruption, and then managing the environment on an ongoing basis.

Services can be taken individually or as a managed arrangement, and can be delivered on Microsoft Azure, Microsoft 365, Google Workspace or other leading cloud platforms depending on your applications, users and data.

  • Cloud storage & file sharing
  • Cloud server / VPS hosting
  • Business email (M365 / Workspace)
  • Migration of servers, files, email & apps
  • Cloud security, monitoring & management
